Privacy information — deployment draft

This is a draft for operators of CreatorPulse. It must be reviewed and updated with the actual company name, jurisdiction, support address, hosting providers and retention policies before a real-user public launch.

What the application processes

When account mode is enabled, CreatorPulse stores email, display name, scrypt password hash, sign-in session tokens as hashes, tracked YouTube video IDs, search keywords, search history, alerts and account plan. If you authorize Google YouTube Analytics, an encrypted OAuth refresh token is stored until you disconnect or delete your account.

How information is used

Account authentication, keyword ranking history, individual creator analytics and in-app alerts. YouTube Data API metadata is fetched from the official API and cached temporarily. No data is sold or shared for advertising in this starter implementation.

Account controls

In account settings you can disconnect Google, revoke extension tokens or permanently delete your account and its stored creator records. Contact information and independent GDPR/DPDP rights workflows must be provided by the deployment operator.

External providers

Google and YouTube services have their own policies. If payments are activated, Stripe/Razorpay process payment details on their own sites; CreatorPulse should store only signed payment/subscription records. Optional local Ollama runs on the operator's machine.

Do not launch real-user accounts using this draft unchanged.
